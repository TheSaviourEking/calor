# calo. studio shoot guide

For anyone shooting calo.'s own product photos: bestsellers, hero shots, the size-beside-a-hand shot. The look is warm and calm, golden light on silk and satin, never explicit.

**One rule: only the background changes, never the product.** Do not retouch the product's colour or shape.

## Backdrops

Three surfaces, the same ones automatic composites use:

- Ivory satin
- Sand linen
- Blush

They are the same images uploaded to storage at `backdrops/satin.jpg`, `backdrops/linen.jpg` and `backdrops/blush.jpg` (at least 2400 px square). Shoot on matching fabric so studio shots sit naturally beside composites.

## Light

- One large, soft key light at 45 degrees to the product.
- Warm white balance, about 4300 K.
- A fill card opposite the key to lift the shadows gently.

## Camera

- At product height, not looking down on it.
- 50 to 85 mm equivalent lens.
- f/8, so the whole product is sharp.

## Required shots

1. **Hero:** the product on satin.
2. **Detail:** texture and material up close.
3. **Scale:** the product beside a real hand. No rings or nail art, neutral and skin-safe framing.
4. **Shipping box:** the plain box exactly as the customer receives it.

## Export and upload

- JPEG, sRGB, long edge 3000 px.
- Each file must be under **5 MB**, the upload limit. Pick an export quality that keeps a 3000 px JPEG under it (around 85 to 90 usually does); a larger file is rejected.
- Upload on the new-product form in admin (`/admin/products/new`) with **"Studio shot — use as is"** ticked. That form is the only place studio uploads can be made today. These images are not processed.
- Check the colour against the real product before uploading. If it looks off, reshoot rather than correct it.
